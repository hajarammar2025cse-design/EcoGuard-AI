Hazard domains

Monitored environmental conditions

🌊

River & flood monitoring

Continuous tracking of river water levels, rainfall rates, and catchment saturation to identify flash flood risks before thresholds are breached.

🔥

Forest fire weather

Analysis of ambient thermal loads, relative humidity, wind velocity, and vegetation dryness to highlight high-danger forest ignition zones.

🌫️

Air quality & smog

Real-time measurement of fine particulates (PM2.5, PM10) and chemical concentrations to calculate standardized AQI and issue health advisories.

How it works

Four steps from sensor to action

1

Sensor telemetry

Stations record physical weather, hydrological, and air readings continuously.

2

Risk evaluation

Data is normalized and evaluated against calibrated safety thresholds.

3

Graded warnings

Advisories are prioritized from "All clear" to "Act now" for fast response.

4

Live overview

Authorities inspect station conditions on GIS maps and export audit reports.
